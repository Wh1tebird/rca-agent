## Invariant evidence

1. `EpisodeRunner` owns lifecycle, admission, query execution, Observation issuance, evidence
   binding, Core decision, budget, stop, Handoff, projections and Answer Freeze. The lifecycle,
   budget, illegal transition, and non-core decision tests pass.
2. LangGraph is routing/projection only. Tests cover Runner-owned preview/frontier rebuilding,
   stale preview rejection, read-only preview, cache-only legacy fields, and no direct tool call.
3. `ReasoningAdapter` and legacy wrappers return typed proposals/failures; authority fields and
   non-JSON context are rejected. A worker-backed adapter can be replaced without changing the
   Runner contract.
4. Evidence providers receive typed, bounded queries and return neutral observations. Capability,
   entity, time, result, cost and budget checks pass; SQL/path-shaped inputs are rejected.
5. Observations are immutable, episode-scoped and provenance-bound. Candidate-scoped admission,
   cross-candidate leak rejection, counter-evidence, and `UNKNOWN`/`MISSING`/`IMPUTED`/
   `MODEL_INFERRED` handling pass. Only trusted observed/derived facts can confirm a candidate.
6. Core is the only decision/stop authority. Insufficient evidence remains `INCONCLUSIVE`;
   out-of-scope and query-failed paths remain explicit statuses; no-valid-query produces Handoff.
7. Worker startup/request deadlines, cancel, exception, crash, terminate, kill, wait/reap and
   malformed/late-result rejection pass. Late results cannot append to Transcript, Workspace,
   Observation ledger or a frozen Answer.
8. Transcript sequence/digest-chain validation, Answer digest immutability, Recorded Replay and
   Recovery (`RESUMABLE`, `RETRYABLE`, `TERMINAL`, `FROZEN`, `CORRUPTED`) pass. Recovery reports
   pending operations and does not guess their outcome or invoke a provider.
9. Audit projection removes secrets, tokens, paths, prompts and raw identifiers. Operational
   metrics are observational and do not change decisions.
10. Evaluation is post-freeze and separate from Runtime artifacts. EvaluationReport and layered
    Failure Taxonomy tests pass without loading evaluator data before freeze.

  directory, not live production telemetry or benchmark quality.

  returns an explicit retry plan for pending operations.

  acceptance boundary.
- No accuracy, recall, benchmark, or Ground Truth claim follows from this acceptance.
